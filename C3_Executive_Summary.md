# C³ — Client-based Cryptographic Cloak
**Status:** Investor-ready concept + runnable local demo  
**Founder:** Dog5pk (Kevin Richardson)  
**Tagline:** A new standard for privacy is on the horizon.

## What C³ is
A privacy cloak that lives on the user’s device. It reshapes and reroutes traffic so patterns can’t be profiled. Client-first, protocol-agnostic, drop-in above apps and below the network.

### Core modules
- **D-Bridge™ mixnet** — multi-hop relays, timing/size obfuscation, offline peer relay.  
- **Local Decoy Injection** — ambient chaff to defeat traffic fingerprinting.  
- **Stealth Domain Support** — Tor/I2P-style compatibility and private bridges.  
- **Adaptive rings** — tunable anonymity sets for future chain interop.  
- **Self-destructing addresses** — short-lived contact/wallet exchange.

## Why now
AI-driven traffic analysis has outgrown legacy VPNs. C³ fights where the signal begins: the client.

## Product & GTM
