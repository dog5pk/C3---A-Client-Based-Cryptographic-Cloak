# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability
- Email: Dog5pkPrrsents@proton.me
- Include: steps to reproduce, affected commit/version, OS/env, and impact.
- Do **not** open public issues or PRs with exploit details.

## Response Targets
- Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours.
- Initial status/update within 7 days.
- Aim to remediate critical issues within 30 days (or share a clear plan).

## Scope
- Relay binaries (`bin_relay1`, `bin_relay2`, `bin_upstream`)
- Harness scripts (`run_local.sh`, `stop_local.sh`, `start_chain.sh`, `stop_chain.sh`)
- Any configs or client code in this repo

## Safe Harbor
Good-faith research that respects privacy, avoids data exfiltration, and does not degrade service will not face legal action. Coordinated disclosure is expected.

## Credit
We publicly credit researchers upon request after a fix or mitigation is available.
